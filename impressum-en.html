General principle

GoldPremium.org has no user accounts, no forms and no newsletter. We do not collect personal data ourselves beyond what is technically necessary. However, when you open the site, certain data (in particular your IP address) is transmitted for technical reasons to the service providers named below.

Website hosting

The website is served via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). GitHub logs access data on its servers (IP address, time, page requested). We cannot access this data and do not evaluate it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and reliable delivery of the site). Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cloudflare (network, protection and audience measurement)

Requests to the website and to the price interface (api.goldpremium.org) are routed through the network of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare forwards the requests, protects the site against attacks and overload (including by limiting the number of requests per IP address) and processes your IP address and technical connection data for this purpose.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics to find out how many pages are viewed, which pages visitors come from and how fast the site loads. For this, a small script from Cloudflare is loaded. It sets no cookies, uses no identifiers to recognise you and creates no user profiles; we only see aggregated figures.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in security, stability and privacy-friendly audience measurement). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can object to the processing, for example by using an ad or script blocker or by email to us. Further information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Price interface (server)

The price data is delivered by our own server at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. It receives requests via Cloudflare. A visitor counter there only counts the total number of page views, without storing IP addresses or other personal data.

External software libraries

For the charts, the website loads software libraries from cdnjs (operated by Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted to these providers. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in reliable display of the content).

Storage in your browser

This website does not set cookies. Your language setting and a few recently loaded values are stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so that the site appears as usual on your next visit. This data does not leave your device and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings. The storage is necessary to provide the display you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

External data sources

Price data is obtained from external sources (dealers, exchanges, interfaces). If our own price interface temporarily delivers no data, the site retrieves gold and silver prices directly from gold-api.com and exchange rates from frankfurter.dev as a fallback. For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted to these providers. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in an available price display). GoldPremium.org accepts no liability for the accuracy of this data.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Please contact: contact@goldpremium.org

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
